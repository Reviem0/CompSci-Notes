# COMP3204 Computer Vision Resources

## Knowledge

- Lecture slides (COMP3204, Southampton)
  The primary source. Every lesson follows the slide content and wording, because the exam is set from them.
- [Book: _Feature Extraction and Image Processing for Computer Vision_, 4th ed., Nixon & Aguado](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/feature-extraction-and/9780128149775/)
  The course text, cited on the slides (lecture 1 = pp. 1–28). Use for: deeper explanation of any lecture topic.
- [Webvision (Univ. of Utah)](https://webvision.med.utah.edu/)
  Authoritative retina reference, used for the slide figures. Use for: rod/cone density, spectral responses.
- [HyperPhysics: The Eye](http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/vision/rfreye.html)
  Concise optics of the eye. Use for: image formation and lens.
- [ACM SIGGRAPH HyperVis: Lateral Inhibition and Adaptation](https://education.siggraph.org/static/HyperVis/vision/latinib.htm)
  Use for: the mechanism behind Mach bands.
- [NYU Perception lecture notes: Brightness & Contrast](https://www.cns.nyu.edu/~david/courses/perception/lecturenotes/brightness-contrast/brightness-contrast.html)
  Use for: Weber's law, brightness perception.
- [CVOnline (Edinburgh)](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/rbf/CVonline/books.htm) · [The DSP Guide](https://www.dspguide.com/)
  Recommended on the slides. Use for: broader CV and signal-processing background.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Module lab sessions and the lecturers' office hours. Use for: checking exam-style answers against what the lecturers expect.
- [r/computervision](https://www.reddit.com/r/computervision/). Use for: practitioner perspective (lower priority given the exam mission).
