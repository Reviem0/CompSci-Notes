Headline question: is human vision a good model for CV?

Lecture's points: (1) three sections · (2) works very well · (3) can be deceived · (4) good model?
For: proof that robust vision is possible; borrow RGB channels, blue compensation, log response, edges and context.
Against: deceived by illusions; brain not understood; different hardware (moving lens, uniform sensor, no blind spot).
Verdict: a source of inspiration, not a blueprint.
