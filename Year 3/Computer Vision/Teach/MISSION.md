# Mission: COMP3204 Computer Vision

## Why
Get a top mark in the COMP3204 exam at Southampton. Every lesson is aimed at exam-style recall and worked problems drawn from the actual lecture slides.

## Success looks like
- Explain each lecture's core ideas from memory, in the module's own terminology
- Work through exam-style calculations and short-answer questions without notes
- Hold a one-page reference sheet per topic that covers what the exam can ask

## Constraints
- Lessons follow the lecture order (XC weeks 2–5, HK weeks 6–10)
- Lecture slides are the primary source; external sources back them up

## Out of scope
- Coursework implementation detail, unless it helps with the exam
- Research-level CV beyond the syllabus
